*** 1. Reaching 0 Stamina
When your Stamina hits 0, you fall *unconscious* and are *dying*.
-   Excess damage from the hit that brought you to 0 is discarded (unless it triggers instant death).
-   You cannot take actions, maneuvers, or move.

*** 2. Death Saves (End of Turn)
At the end of each of your turns while dying, you must make a *Death Save* (a flat 1d10 roll):
-   *10:* You stabilize. You are no longer dying, but you remain unconscious at 0 Stamina.
-   *2–9:* Nothing happens. You remain dying.
-   *1:* You take 1 *Death Mark*.

*** 3. Dying
You die if any of the following occur:
-   You accrue *3 Death Marks*.
-   You take damage equal to your *maximum Stamina* in a single hit while at 0.
-   An enemy uses an action to execute you (usually reserved for villains in dramatic moments).

*** 4. Getting Back Up
-   *Healing:* If you regain any Stamina, you immediately regain consciousness and lose all Death Marks.
-   *Stabilizing:* An ally can use an action to stabilize you (usually an Oversight or Medicine-based test). If successful, you stop making death saves but stay at 0 Stamina and unconscious.

*** 5. The "Heroic Stand"
The system encourages cinematic moments. Certain abilities or Heroic Resources might allow a character at 0 Stamina to take one final, dramatic turn before falling or potentially staying in the fight through sheer grit.

*** 2. Death
You die if your Stamina reaches the *negative of your Winded Value*.
-   *Winded Value:* This number is equal to half your maximum Stamina.
-   /Example:/ If your Max Stamina is 40, your Winded Value is 20. You are dying when you hit 0 Stamina. You die if you reach *-20* Stamina.

*** 3. Stabilization and Recovery
Since you cannot use /Catch Breath/ while dying, you generally cannot heal yourself without a specific ability or potion. However:
-   *Allied Help:* An ally adjacent to you can use a maneuver to *Help* you. This allows you to spend a *Recovery*, regaining Stamina equal to your recovery value (1/3 your Max Stamina) and ending the dying condition.
-   *Healing Abilities:* Any magical healing from allies (like a Conduit's prayers) that raises your Stamina above 0 will stop you from dying.

*** Summary
In Draw Steel, hitting 0 Stamina is a moment of heroic desperation. You can choose to make a final stand (taking actions and suffering bleeding damage close to true death) or retreat and hope an ally can get to you before enemy attacks finish you off.

*** Non-Lethal Option
If an attacker deals damage that would kill a creature, they can choose to *knock them unconscious* instead. An unconscious creature wakes up with 1 Stamina after 1 hour (or after spending a recovery). If they take damage while unconscious in this way, they die.
